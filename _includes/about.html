About Me

Portrait @ Nikolaus Brade

I am a physician scientist working at the intersection of neuroimaging and the molecular and cellular biology of the human brain. I trained in medicine at RWTH Aachen University and obtained my doctorate (Dr. med.) with a thesis on resting-state functional MRI in anorexia nervosa.

I am a research associate at the Institute of Neuroscience and Medicine: Brain and Behaviour (INM-7), Forschungszentrum Jülich, and the Institute of Systems Neuroscience, University Hospital Düsseldorf, where I work in Jürgen Dukart's Biomarker Development Group. Alongside, I am completing an additional PhD in the Clinician Scientist Program of the Max Planck School of Cognition.
